UniHop moves packages the last mile. You send us a pickup, a dropoff, and when it needs to happen. We source a driver, and our dispatch team watches the delivery through to the doorstep.

There are two ways to integrate. Pick the one that matches who owns the orders.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Partner API" icon="building" href="/partners/index">
    For a business booking its own deliveries, and for marketplaces booking for the sellers they host. You get an account of your own, signed-JWT authentication, and one bill for everything through the integration.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Platform API" icon="puzzle-piece" href="/platforms/index">
    For software that other businesses install and book through, like a checkout scheduling app or an order management system. Each merchant uses their own API key and is billed on their own account.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Both integrations share

The same delivery engine, the same dispatch team, and the same delivery styles. Requests and responses use the same field names, and both can receive webhooks as a delivery moves.

What differs is who authenticates, and who pays.

## Need a hand?

Your account manager handles accounts, delivery styles, and coverage. Integration questions: [help.unihop.app](https://help.unihop.app).
