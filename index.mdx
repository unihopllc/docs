UniHop moves packages the last mile. You send us a pickup, a dropoff, and when it needs to happen. We source a driver, and our dispatch team watches the delivery through to the doorstep.

Two APIs. Pick the one that matches whose account the deliveries are booked on.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Direct API" icon="building" href="/direct/index">
    **Book on your own account.** One signed-JWT credential, one bill for everything you book. For a business booking its own deliveries, and for marketplaces booking for the merchants they host, with an optional `merchant_id` to attribute each order to the right one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Platform API" icon="puzzle-piece" href="/platforms/index">
    **Book on your merchants' accounts.** Each merchant brings their own UniHop API key and is billed directly, so you are never in the billing path. For software other businesses install and book through, like a checkout scheduling app or an order management system.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Both APIs share

The same delivery engine, the same dispatch team, and the same delivery styles. Requests and responses use the same field names, and both can receive webhooks as a delivery moves.

What differs is who authenticates, and who pays.

## Need a hand?

Your account manager handles accounts, delivery styles, and coverage. API questions: [help.unihop.app](https://help.unihop.app).
